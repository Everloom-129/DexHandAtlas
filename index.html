About this work

Independent research. DexHand Atlas is a personal, independent research project by Jie Wang — not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any vendor listed. It exists to map a fast-moving field in one place. All trademarks, product names, and images belong to their respective owners; images are linked or reproduced for reference and identification only.
How to read it. Figures are vendor- or paper-reported and inconsistently defined (active DOF vs. total joints; fingertip vs. whole-hand force; list vs. quote-only prices). Treat single-number comparisons as approximate, and confirm anything decision-critical with the original source — every entry links back to one.
