Hands on the Market

Multi-finger and anthropomorphic robotic & prosthetic hands available to buy, with reference prices and product images.

14 products USD reference prices compiled June 1, 2026
Read the prices as orders of magnitude, not quotes. Most research-grade hands are built-to-order; published figures range widely. Where a vendor lists "price on request," a 3rd-party reference range is shown and tagged ref.. Single-hand pricing unless stated; pairs and tactile ("-P") versions cost more. Prices are list / 3rd-party reference prices in USD unless noted and vary by configuration, region, tactile options, and academic vs. commercial licensing. Always confirm with the vendor.

Beyond this list

The 14 above are the most commonly purchased / cited hands with public pricing. The market is much larger; the Humanoid.guide hands directory tracks 70+ models, including Seed Robotics RH8D/RH6D, AgiBot OmniHand, PaXini DexH13 / GMH18, WIRobotics ALLEX, Shadow DEX-EE (3-finger RL hand), Open Bionics Hero, Prensilia Mia / IH2 Azzurra, Robotis HX5, OYMotion ROHand, ETH ORCA, Sanctuary Phoenix hand, DexRobot DexHand021, and many more, most quoted "price on request."
