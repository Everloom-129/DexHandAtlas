Dexterous Hand
Benchmarks

A literature review of how the field measures the hand, the grasp, and the policy.
Scope  benchmarks · datasets · devices · VLAs Compiled  Jun 2026 Anchors  DexYCB · DexArt · POMDAR Entries  24 systems

The two starting references sit in two different paradigms. DexYCB (NVIDIA, CVPR 2021) is a perception dataset for capturing hand grasping of objects, built for pose estimation and human-to-robot handover. DexArt (UC San Diego, CVPR 2023) is a simulation reinforcement-learning benchmark for generalizable manipulation of articulated objects with a multi-finger hand. Most of the field splits along that axis, plus a third cluster that has become much more active recently: benchmarks that measure the device itself rather than any policy running on it — anchored by POMDAR (ETH Zurich, 2026), which scores a hand's dexterity as task throughput over a taxonomy of manipulation and grasp motions.

This dossier organizes the space into six paradigms, traces the timeline from 2021 to 2026, compares the hardware on degrees of freedom, and closes with the gaps that matter from an evaluation-infrastructure standpoint.

01

Hardware-dexterity benchmarks

The hand as a device

The freshest cluster, and the one closest to evaluation-infrastructure work, because it measures the device rather than a policy. The recurring complaint is that hands get compared on proxy metrics like degrees of freedom or reachable workspace, which describe potential but not performance.

POMDAR

ETH Zurich · Soft Robotics Lab (Katzschmann) · arXiv 2604.09294 · Apr 2026

Formalizes dexterity as task performance across a structured set of manipulation and grasping motions derived from human-motor-control taxonomies (Elliott & Connolly, Ma & Dollar, Feix GRASP). Implemented in both real-world and simulation across four configurations: vertical, horizontal, continuous rotation, and pure grasping.

Two transferable primitives: mechanical scaffolds that constrain motion to the intended DoF and suppress compensatory strategies (gravity assist, palm support, excess wrist motion), and a single score combining task correctness with execution speed, treating dexterity as throughput. Fully 3D-printable and open source.

ORCA-hand dexterity benchmark

arXiv 2604.09294-adjacent · 2026

Evaluates ORCA-hand embodiments with degrees of freedom locked and unlocked from 5 to 16 DoF. The largest performance gaps appear on tasks requiring coordinated multi-finger interaction and finger abduction (squeeze, palmar, chopsticks), which is direct evidence that DoF count alone is a poor dexterity proxy.

MFR_benchmark · Elliott & Connolly lineage

UMich ARM Lab · plus classic E&C, 50 HD-marks, Dexterity Test Board

MFR is a multi-finger reorientation suite (valve turning, cuboid insertion, screwdriver turning, object reorientation) implemented on the Allegro hand. POMDAR catalogs the older task-performance benchmarks worth citing for lineage.

02

Perception datasets

Where DexYCB lives

DexYCB

NVIDIA (Chao, Fox et al.) · CVPR 2021 · arXiv 2104.04631

A dataset for capturing hand grasping of objects, benchmarking three tasks: 2D object and keypoint detection, 6D object pose estimation, and 3D hand pose estimation. It also defines a robotics task: generating safe robot grasps in human-to-robot handover. CC BY-NC 4.0, with an open evaluation toolkit. This is a data and perception benchmark, not a control benchmark.

03

Grasp synthesis and grasp evaluation

Does the grasp hold, and at what scale

DexGraspNet

He Wang / Hao Su lineage · large-scale ShadowHand grasp dataset

The standard large-scale dexterous grasp dataset for synthesis evaluation, widely used as a training and benchmarking source for grasp generators.

Get a Grip

Stanford (Lum et al., Bohg lab) · arXiv 2410.23701 · 2024

Multi-finger grasp evaluation at scale, explicitly framed around enabling robust sim-to-real transfer of grasp quality prediction.

GraspGen · DextrAH-RGB

NVIDIA Research (Singh, Handa, Van Wyk et al.) · 2024–2025

GraspGen is a diffusion-based 6-DOF grasping framework backed by a synthetic dataset of over 57 million grasps across robots and grippers. DextrAH-RGB is a sim-to-real workflow for dexterous grasping from stereo RGB input, from the same group that built DexYCB.

04

Simulation RL and policy benchmarks

DexArt's family

DexArt

UC San Diego (Bao, Xu, Qin, Wang) · CVPR 2023 · arXiv 2305.05706

Dexterous manipulation of articulated objects (faucets, laptops, buckets, toilets) in a physics simulator, evaluating generalization to unseen articulated objects and robustness to camera viewpoint change. Uses PPO with a PointNet backbone on point-cloud input. Key finding: 3D geometric representation learning gives strong robustness to viewpoint shift where ResNet-based policies collapse.

Bi-DexHands

Built on Isaac Gym

Tasks calibrated to human-motor-skill levels from cognitive-science literature, reaching 30,000+ FPS on a single RTX 3090. Provides a comprehensive benchmark across single-agent, multi-agent, offline, multi-task, and meta RL settings.

DexMimicGen

NVIDIA GEAR + UT Austin + UCSD (Jiang, Mandlekar, Fan, Zhu) · ICRA 2025 · arXiv 2410.24185

A simulation environment suite as much as a data pipeline. Generates 21K demonstrations from just 60 source human demos across bimanual dexterous tasks, spanning independent, coordinated, and sequential two-arm subtasks, with a real-to-sim-to-real deployment loop. The bimanual successor to MimicGen and a natural reference point for competitive analysis.

05

VLAs and foundation models on dexterous hands

Where the eval target is moving

The evaluation target is shifting from task-specific policies to generalist models running on dexterous embodiments. This matters because failure modes change and attribution gets harder.

DexVLA

Yichen Zhu et al. · arXiv 2502.05855 · 2025

A billion-parameter diffusion action expert with embodiment curriculum learning, tested across single-arm, bimanual, and dexterous-hand embodiments, reported to beat Octo, OpenVLA, and Diffusion Policy.

DexGraspVLA

Zhong et al. · arXiv 2502.20900 · 2025

A hierarchical framework using a pretrained VLM as high-level planner plus a diffusion-based low-level controller for language-guided general dexterous grasping.

GR00T N1 · Gemini Robotics · π0.5

NVIDIA · Google DeepMind · Physical Intelligence · 2025

The generalist humanoid and dexterous foundation models. π0 and π0.5 use flow matching for continuous action prediction enabling dexterous manipulation at up to 50 Hz; GR00T attends to the last VLM layer via cross attention.

TRI Large Behavior Models — dexterity eval

Toyota Research Institute · 2025 · "A Careful Examination of LBMs for Multitask Dexterous Manipulation"

An evaluation-methodology paper rather than a new model. Recent challenge writeups echo its theme: dexterity issues, clumsiness in grasping and releasing, dominate VLA failure modes ahead of progress and order errors and out-of-distribution confusion. This failure-attribution framing is the most directly relevant to counterfactual episode recording for eval.

06

Hardware devices: research and frontier companies

The substrate everything runs on

LEAP Hand / LEAP V2

CMU (Shaw, Agarwal, Pathak) · RSS 2023 · arXiv 2309.06440

Assembled in 4 hours for 2000 USD from off-the-shelf parts, with a kinematic structure designed for maximal dexterity regardless of finger pose. Reported to outperform Allegro across experiments at one-eighth the cost. LEAP V2 (the DLA Hand) adds a hybrid rigid-soft build with a 3D-printed soft skin over an internal bone structure and a powered foldable palm, around 3000 USD.

Shadow DEX-EE · Shadow Hand

Shadow Robot × Google DeepMind · May 2024

DEX-EE is a modular multi-finger hand built for reinforcement learning, with modular finger units, hundreds of tactile sensors per hand, and durability across thousands of impact cycles. The classic Shadow Hand is 24 DoF, the same as OpenAI's Dactyl.

Teleop and data devices: DOGlove · DexUMI

Tsinghua (arXiv 2502.07730) · arXiv 2505.21864

DOGlove is a low-cost open-source haptic force-feedback glove. DexUMI uses the human hand as a universal manipulation interface, transferring relative finger trajectories that prove more robust to hardware imperfection.

Commercial humanoid hands

Tesla · Figure · Sanctuary · Agility · SCHUNK · 2024–2026

Tesla Optimus went from 11 DoF (Gen 2) to 22 DoF on the hand plus 3 on the wrist, using a tendon-driven design with actuators relocated to the forearm. Treat any spec as provisional: Musk stated in April 2026 that a patented 25-actuator design did not work and Tesla iterated again. Figure 03 reports 24+ DoF hands paired with the Helix platform. Sanctuary uses hydraulic actuation, diverging from the all-electric trend. As of 2026 the field has converged on five-fingered anthropomorphic hands but diverges sharply on actuation, sensing, and control.

07

Gaps worth noting

Evaluation-infrastructure lens

The paradigms do not talk to each other.

Simulation benchmarks (DexArt, Bi-DexHands, DexMimicGen) and hardware-dexterity benchmarks (POMDAR, the Elliott & Connolly lineage) are disconnected. There is no standardized bridge that scores a policy on a specific hand against a taxonomy-grounded dexterity baseline. POMDAR's throughput metric and scaffold methodology are the most transferable primitives to build on.

Failure attribution for dexterous VLAs is still manual.

The TRI and challenge writeups label failures by hand. A benchmark that records why a dexterous grasp failed, with counterfactual rollouts, would be novel and sits directly adjacent to counterfactual episode recording for eval.

Hand-agnostic evaluation is nearly absent.

Almost everything is tied to one embodiment: Allegro for MFR, ShadowHand for DexGraspNet, specific humanoids for the VLAs. Given the DoF churn at Tesla and Figure, an eval format robust across hand morphologies would carry real value.
